Grasping the Future: Joint 3D Hand–Object Forecasting from Internet Videos

Supplementary videos: qualitative predictions for five tasks from a single model.

Each video shows one held-out clip. Top: the observed frame, the input given to the model, and the reference motion. Bottom: the best of 20 predictions and all 20 samples, animated over the dimmed frame. The strip underneath shows which states are observed and which are predicted for each hand and the object.

Observed (given to the model) Predicted Reference motion or given goal

Cases come from held-out MoCap sequences and from Action100M internet videos, where the reference is our extracted pseudo-ground truth. Samples are drawn independently; "best of 20" is the sample closest to the reference. For display, predicted and reference motion is lightly smoothed over time (about 0.1 s), and hands that penetrate the object are shifted rigidly out of it by a few millimetres without lifting fingers that touch it; hand poses and finger articulation are unchanged.
